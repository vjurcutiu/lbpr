# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 29, 2025  

This Privacy Policy explains how **Scipio Systems** ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the **LexBot PRO** chat application ("Service", "App").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Machine ID
When you use our Service, we generate and assign a unique machine ID to your device.  
This machine ID is used to identify your account, manage access to your stored data in our Pinecone index, and for subscription management and payment processing with Stripe.

### Usage Data
We collect limited usage data, including the number of messages sent and files uploaded via the Service.  
This data helps us monitor system usage and ensure fair access.

### Payment Information
Payments are processed via **Stripe**.  
Your machine ID and relevant subscription data are transmitted to Stripe to facilitate billing and subscription management.  
We do **not** store your payment card details or other personal financial information on our servers.

### Uploaded Documents
Any documents you upload to the Service are processed and stored solely for the purpose of providing the chat functionality and related AI search services.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain the Service, including document search and retrieval features.  
- Associate your machine ID with your stored data in Pinecone.  
- Manage your subscription and process payments via Stripe.  
- Monitor usage (messages sent, files uploaded) to maintain fair access and prevent abuse.  
- Improve and secure the Service.

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### Third-Party Services
In order to provide the Service, we use the following third-party providers:

- **OpenAI** — for AI-powered document analysis and chat responses.  
  Documents and user queries may be transmitted to OpenAI servers for processing.  
  See [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy).  

- **Pinecone** — for vector data storage and retrieval.  
  See [Pinecone Privacy Policy](https://www.pinecone.io/privacy).  

- **Stripe** — for payment processing and subscription management.  
  See [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

Data shared with these providers is limited to what is necessary to perform the Service.  
Depending on the provider, data may be processed or stored outside the European Economic Area (EEA), in compliance with applicable law.

### Legal Compliance
We may disclose your information if required by law or to comply with a legal obligation.

### No Sale or Marketing
We do **not** sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or marketers.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as you maintain an active subscription or account with us.  
Upon account termination or at your request, we will delete your machine ID, usage data, and stored documents, subject to technical feasibility and any legal obligations.
