# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 12, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how **Scipio Systems** ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and discloses personal data when you use **LexBot PRO** and related websites, applications, and services (the "Service").

This document describes our privacy practices as the provider of the Service. Where we process customer content on behalf of a business customer, our [Data Processing Agreement](./dpa.md) also applies.

---

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

- our marketing website and application front-end;
- customer accounts, authentication, and billing;
- files, prompts, messages, audio, and other content submitted to the Service;
- support, security, logging, analytics, and operational monitoring.

It does **not** apply to third-party websites, products, or services that you access separately from the Service, which remain governed by their own terms and privacy notices.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect the following categories of information.

### Account and authentication data
We may collect account identifiers and profile information such as name, email address, phone number, login method, account status, and related authentication metadata.  
Authentication and core storage features may be provided through **Firebase** and related **Google Cloud** services.

### Customer content
We process content that you or your authorized users submit to the Service, including uploaded documents, text inputs, prompts, chat messages, file metadata, OCR inputs, audio files, transcripts, and generated outputs.

### Billing and subscription data
If you subscribe to a paid plan, billing and subscription information is processed through **Stripe**.  
We do not store full payment card numbers on our own servers.

### Usage and technical data
We may collect technical and operational data such as IP address, device and browser information, timestamps, request identifiers, error data, performance data, log records, and feature-usage events.

### Analytics, cookies, and marketing data
Where enabled, we may collect analytics, attribution, or consent-related data from our website or application, such as cookie identifiers, browser identifiers, campaign parameters, page and feature events, and similar usage data.  
Where required by law, optional analytics or advertising technologies are used only on the basis of the required consent or other lawful basis.

### Pseudonymization and protected-content workflows
To reduce unnecessary exposure of personal data in downstream processing, we may pseudonymize or tokenise detected personal identifiers within customer content before selected AI, search, or indexing workflows are run. We keep the mapping needed to restore user-facing text under our control and use it to reconstruct authorized outputs in the application before they are shown to the user.  
This is a privacy and security safeguard, not a claim that the data has been irreversibly anonymized. Original uploaded source files may still be stored in encrypted form in **Firebase / Google Cloud** storage in order to support storage, retrieval, and download functionality.

## 3. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data to:

- provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Service;
- authenticate users and manage account access;
- process files, prompts, search requests, OCR, transcription, and AI-generated responses;
- pseudonymize detected identifiers and restore user-facing outputs where applicable;
- store and retrieve indexed data for search and retrieval functionality;
- process payments, subscriptions, invoices, and account administration;
- detect abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and service misuse;
- debug, monitor, log, and improve reliability, performance, and security;
- measure product usage, website traffic, and campaign performance, where enabled;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our contractual rights.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to provide the Service, maintain business and security records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Retention periods may vary by data type. For example:

- account and billing records may be retained for legal, tax, and bookkeeping purposes;
- operational logs and telemetry may be retained for security, debugging, and service reliability purposes;
- customer content is generally retained for the duration of the applicable account or subscription, unless deleted earlier by the customer or required to be retained by law;
- encrypted stored files and token-mapping records may be retained for as long as needed to provide requested storage, retrieval, and user-facing de-pseudonymization functionality, subject to deletion policies and legal obligations.

## 8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

These measures may include access controls, encryption in transit, encrypted cloud storage, secrets management, pseudonymization or tokenization of detected identifiers before selected downstream processing, controlled restoration of user-facing outputs in the application, environment segregation, logging, rate limiting, and vendor security controls.  
No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
