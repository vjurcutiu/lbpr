# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Effective Date:** April 12, 2026  

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between **Scipio Systems** ("Processor", "we", "us", "our") and the customer entity using **LexBot PRO** and related services ("Controller", "Customer", "you", "your").

This DPA is incorporated into and forms part of the agreement governing your use of the Service, including the Terms and Conditions and any applicable order form or subscription terms (the "Agreement").

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This DPA sets out the terms under which we process personal data on your behalf in connection with the Service.

This DPA applies where we process personal data as a processor or subprocessor on your behalf.  
It does not apply to processing for which we act as an independent controller, such as our own business contact records, billing administration, legal compliance, fraud prevention, product-improvement analytics that are not performed on your behalf, and internal business operations.

---

## 2. Roles of the Parties

- You act as the **controller** or, where applicable, a processor acting on behalf of another controller.
- We act as the **processor** or, where applicable, a subprocessor, for customer content and related personal data processed through the Service.

Each party is independently responsible for complying with the data protection obligations that apply to it under applicable law.

---

## 3. Customer Instructions

We will process personal data only:

- to provide the Service in accordance with the Agreement;
- on your documented instructions as set out in the Agreement, this DPA, and your use of the Service;
- as otherwise required by applicable law.

You instruct us to use the subprocessors listed in this DPA and to process personal data as reasonably necessary to provide hosting, authentication, storage, encryption, pseudonymization, indexing, search, AI functionality, billing-related operations, security, logging, telemetry, and customer support relating to the Service.

---

## 4. Customer Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

- ensuring that you have a valid legal basis to collect and provide personal data to us for processing;
- ensuring that your instructions comply with applicable data protection law;
- providing legally required notices to data subjects and obtaining any required consents or authorizations;
- determining whether the Service is appropriate for the personal data you choose to process through it, including any special categories of personal data;
- implementing appropriate access controls and internal policies for your users.

---

## 5. Processor Obligations

We will:

- process personal data only on documented instructions, unless otherwise required by law;
- ensure that persons authorized to process personal data are subject to confidentiality obligations;
- implement reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the processing and the risks presented;
- assist you, taking into account the nature of processing, with reasonable requests relating to data subject rights, security incidents, data protection impact assessments, and consultations with supervisory authorities, where required by law;
- notify you without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed personal data breach affecting personal data processed under this DPA, as required by applicable law.

---

## 6. Security Measures

We maintain technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, which may include as appropriate:

- access controls and least-privilege permissions;
- logical segregation of environments;
- encryption in transit and encrypted cloud storage;
- pseudonymization or tokenization of detected identifiers before selected downstream AI or indexing workflows, with controlled restoration of user-facing outputs in the application;
- logging, monitoring, and rate limiting;
- backup and recovery practices;
- vendor security reviews and contractual protections.

You acknowledge that no security measure is perfect and that the measures must be assessed in light of the nature of the data, the Service configuration you choose, and the risks associated with your use case.

---

## 7. Subprocessors

You authorize us to engage the subprocessors listed in **Annex II** to process personal data on your behalf in connection with the Service.

We will remain responsible for the performance of our subprocessors' obligations to the extent required by applicable law and our contract with you.

We may update our subprocessors from time to time. Where required by law or contract, we will provide notice of material subprocessor changes by updating our public documentation, the Service, or by other reasonable means.

---

## 8. Data Subject Requests

Taking into account the nature of the processing, we will provide reasonable assistance to help you respond to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under applicable data protection law.

If we receive a request directly from a data subject relating to personal data processed on your behalf, we may direct the requester to you unless we are legally required to respond otherwise.

---

## 9. International Transfers

You acknowledge that personal data may be transferred to countries outside the EEA, UK, or Switzerland in order to provide the Service.

Where required, we and our subprocessors will rely on lawful transfer mechanisms such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, or other recognized safeguards.  
Transfers are not based on data-subject consent unless expressly stated otherwise by the relevant parties and legally required.

---

## 10. Return and Deletion

Upon termination or expiration of the Service, and subject to the Agreement, applicable law, and standard backup/retention practices, we will delete or return personal data processed under this DPA within a reasonable period, unless retention is required by law.

You acknowledge that certain residual copies may remain in backups, logs, or archival systems for a limited period, after which they will be deleted in the ordinary course.

---

## 11. Audit and Information Rights

Upon reasonable written request and subject to confidentiality, security, and proportionality requirements, we will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA.

Where appropriate, we may satisfy audit or information requests by providing current third-party audit reports, security documentation, certifications, or comparable materials made available by us or our subprocessors.

---

## 12. Liability

The liability of each party under this DPA is subject to the exclusions and limitations of liability in the Agreement, except to the extent such limitations are prohibited by applicable law.

Nothing in this DPA relieves either party of its own direct responsibilities under applicable data protection law.

---

## 13. Governing Law

This DPA is governed by the governing law and dispute resolution provisions set out in the Agreement, unless applicable data protection law requires otherwise.

## Annex I — Details of Processing

### A. Subject Matter
Provision of an AI-enabled document and knowledge assistant, including authentication, storage, retrieval, search, chat, OCR, transcription, indexing, billing support, security, and related support services.

### B. Duration
For the duration of the Agreement and any limited retention period required for backups, legal compliance, security, billing, or dispute resolution.

### C. Nature and Purpose of Processing
Collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, retrieval, consultation, use, transmission, alignment, pseudonymization, encrypted storage, analysis, deletion, and destruction of personal data as necessary to provide and secure the Service.

### D. Categories of Data Subjects
Depending on Customer's use of the Service:

- Customer account owners, administrators, employees, contractors, and authorized users;
- individuals whose personal data appears in documents, prompts, messages, audio, or other customer content;
- billing and support contacts.

### E. Categories of Personal Data
Depending on Customer's use of the Service:

- names, email addresses, phone numbers, user IDs, authentication identifiers, and profile data;
- uploaded files and document contents;
- prompts, chat messages, search inputs, transcripts, and outputs;
- file metadata, usage records, logs, device/browser data, IP addresses, and request identifiers;
- billing, subscription, and transaction metadata.

### F. Special Categories / Sensitive Data
The Service is not intended for special categories of personal data unless Customer determines that such use is lawful and appropriate and implements any additional controls required by law.  
If Customer chooses to process special categories of personal data through the Service, Customer is responsible for ensuring an appropriate legal basis and any required safeguards.
