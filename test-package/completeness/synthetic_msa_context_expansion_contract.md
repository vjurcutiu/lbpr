# Exhibit A — Data Handling and Restricted Data Controls

## A.1 Data Classification
Customer Data is classified as follows:

1. **Public Data:** material intentionally made public by Customer.  
2. **Internal Data:** ordinary business information not intended for public disclosure.  
3. **Confidential Data:** non-public commercial, operational, legal, technical, or financial information.  
4. **Restricted Data:** trade secrets, customer purchase history, customer lists, employee records, credentials, legal privilege material, security logs, payment information, and data identified in an SOW as restricted.

If data could reasonably fit multiple classes, the more protective class applies.

## A.2 Restricted Data Handling
Provider must restrict access to Restricted Data to personnel with a business need, maintain access logs, use encryption in transit and at rest, and prohibit copying Restricted Data into development, demo, public support, training, analytics, or benchmarking environments. Restricted Data may be used in a support environment only if Customer provides written approval or the data has been redacted or anonymized.

## A.3 Customer Data in AI Features
Provider may process Customer Data through AI features to provide Customer-requested workflows. Provider may not use Restricted Data for model training, model evaluation, public benchmarking, synthetic data generation, or general product demonstrations. Internal evaluation of Customer-specific workflow quality is permitted only where the evaluation is limited to Customer’s tenant, access-controlled, and deleted according to Section 9.7.

## A.4 Competitor Use Controls
If an SOW permits Competitor Use, Provider must create a separate workspace, restrict access to Customer-approved users, disable sharing outside the workspace, prevent cross-tenant prompt or output reuse, and disable Derived Insights that could expose Customer-specific pricing, merchandising, supplier, or customer segmentation patterns.

## A.5 Support Materials
Support Materials containing Restricted Data must be marked as Restricted Data or reasonably treated as such based on context. Provider must delete Restricted Data in Support Materials within thirty days after the ticket is closed unless retention is legally required or needed for incident investigation.

## A.6 Prohibited Data
Unless expressly approved in an SOW, Customer must not submit payment card numbers, government identifiers, health data, children’s data, biometric identifiers, authentication secrets, or production security keys. If Customer submits prohibited data, Provider may quarantine or delete it and may suspend affected workflows.
